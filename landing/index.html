Lifecycleone booking, confirm to settlement

Four entries, two of them on-chain.

This is a real booking, in the order the ledger records it. The money is locked before the Mac is told anything, and released only against measured usage.

t+0.00s open

Escrow locks

The consumer confirms a duration and the full reserved amount moves into ClawEscrow. The supplier's Mac has not heard about the booking yet — the funds go first.

t+0.11s dispatch

The Mac gets the job

The marketplace pushes the booking down the worker's own outbound WebSocket. Nothing listens on the supplier's machine, so nothing has to be opened to the internet.

t+0.94s ready

Sandbox is up

A fresh microVM boots and MLX preloads the model. Nothing from the previous renter survives the teardown — no filesystem, no cache, no model state.

t+50m settle

Settlement for time used

The session ends ten minutes early. The contract pays the supplier 85% of the fifty minutes consumed, keeps 15%, and returns the unused remainder to the consumer in the same transaction.

Only open and settle touch the chain. Everything between them is the worker talking to the marketplace over a socket it opened itself.
